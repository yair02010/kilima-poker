We never sell personal data.

Per the Data Retention Policy (KP-LEG-06): financial and game records for the period required by gaming, AML and tax laws (typically 5–7 years after account closure), shorter periods for other data.

# 7. Your Rights

Access, correction, deletion (subject to legal retention), objection, restriction, portability (your hand histories and statements), withdrawal of consent, complaint to the data-protection authority [name per country]. Contact: [DPO email]. We answer within [30 days / legal deadline].

# 8. Security

Encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, monitoring, and regular independent testing (KP-SEC-02). We will notify you and the authorities of breaches as the law requires.

# 9. Children

Kilima Poker is for adults only ([18+ or local minimum]). We delete data of minors discovered on the platform, except where retention is legally required.

# 10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Web: essential cookies (login, security), preferences, and analytics with consent where required. App: device identifiers for security and analytics; push tokens.

# 11. Changes
