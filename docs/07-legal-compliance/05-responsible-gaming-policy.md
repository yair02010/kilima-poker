# 1. Commitment

Poker must remain entertainment. Kilima Poker designs its product, operations and marketing to prevent harm, protect minors and vulnerable people, and help players stay in control.

# 2. Player Tools

@widths 1.5,3.5
| Tool | Details |
|---|---|
| Deposit limits | Daily, weekly, monthly; decreases immediate, increases after 24 h (or longer if required) |
| Loss limits | Net loss per period across cash and tournaments |
| Session time limits | Maximum session length; automatic stand-up after current hands |
| Reality checks | Every 60 minutes by default (configurable 15–120): time played, net result, option to stop |
| Cool-off | 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days; cannot be undone early |
| Self-exclusion | 6 months, 1 year, 5 years, permanent; network-wide across operators sharing the identity; marketing stops; national registers integrated where they exist |
| Activity statement | Deposits, withdrawals, net result, time played by period |
| Blocking-software guidance | Information on device-level gambling blockers |

Tools are available in Play Money too, and are reachable within two taps from any screen.

# 3. Protection by Design

- No credit: players cannot play with borrowed money from Kilima; no negative balances.
- No "near miss" or misleading design; Spin multipliers and RTP disclosed.
- Clocks and session timers visible at tables; no autoplay of deposits.
- Fast formats (fast-fold, Spins) show session summaries more frequently.

# 4. Detecting Harm

- Markers: increasing deposits after losses, repeated failed deposits, cancelling withdrawals to keep playing, long or late-night sessions, rapid limit increases, chasing losses, contacting support in distress.
- A risk model scores players daily (KP-ENG-16 §4); player care reviews high scores and intervenes: supportive message, suggestion of limits or cool-off, phone/WhatsApp contact, and, where required or clearly appropriate, mandatory measures.
- Every intervention and outcome is recorded.

# 5. Staff

- Training at onboarding and yearly; player-care specialists receive advanced training.
- Support agents apply cool-off or self-exclusion immediately at the player's request and escalate signs of harm.
- Staff incentives are never tied to player losses or deposits.

# 6. Marketing Rules

- No marketing to under-18s, self-excluded or cooling-off players, or players flagged by the risk model.
- No suggestion that poker solves financial problems or is a source of income; no pressure tactics.
- Responsible-gaming message and age limit in all advertising; local advertising codes respected.
- Influencers and affiliates contractually bound to these rules.

# 7. Help Resources

Each market has a list of local and international support organisations and helplines, maintained by player care, verified quarterly, and shown in the app in local languages.
