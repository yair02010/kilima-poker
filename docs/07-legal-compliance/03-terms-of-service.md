# 2. Key Clauses (draft wording)

- **Eligibility:** "You must be at least [18] years old (or the legal age in your country, if higher), located in a country where we are permitted to offer our games to you, and not self-excluded. Our employees and their household members may not play for real money."
- **One account:** "You may hold only one account across the Kilima network. Accounts opened in breach of this rule may be closed and winnings from them withheld where permitted by law."
- **Own funds and instruments:** "You may deposit only from payment methods in your own name and withdraw only to verified methods in your own name."
- **Prohibited software and conduct:** "You must not use bots, real-time assistance tools, solvers, shared hand information, collusion, chip dumping, account sharing, or any tool that gives you an unfair advantage while playing. A list of prohibited and permitted tools is in the House Rules."
- **Investigations:** "We may suspend play and hold funds while we investigate suspected breaches. If we confirm a breach, we may close your account and withhold winnings obtained unfairly, and we may redistribute them to affected players. Deposits not obtained through the breach are returned to you, subject to our legal obligations."
- **Malfunctions:** "A malfunction voids affected hands and plays. Chips committed to a voided hand are returned. Tournament issues are handled under the House Rules."
- **Disconnection:** "We are not responsible for losses caused by your device or internet connection. Hands continue while you are disconnected."
- **Taxes:** "Where the law requires, we withhold taxes from winnings or deposits and pay them to the tax authority; your statement shows the tax withheld."
- **Dormancy:** "If you do not log in for [12] months, we will contact you. We do not charge dormancy fees [unless permitted and stated here]. Balances remain yours and are handled as the law requires."
- **Complaints:** "Contact support first. If you are not satisfied, you may escalate to [our complaints team] and then to [the regulator / ADR body], as provided by [law]."
