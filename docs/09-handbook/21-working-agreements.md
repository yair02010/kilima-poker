# 1. Working Agreements

1. **Write it down.** Decisions happen in writing (ticket, RFC, ADR, PR). Meetings end with written outcomes.
2. **Async first, meetings with purpose.** Every meeting has an agenda and an owner; no-meeting blocks Tuesday and Thursday mornings for focus.
3. **Public by default.** Ask and answer in team channels, not DMs, unless it is personal or security-sensitive.
4. **Time zones.** Core collaboration hours 11:00–15:00 EAT (UTC+3); everything else async.
5. **Blameless.** We fix systems, not people. Mistakes are reported early and without fear.
6. **Customers and players first.** When in doubt, protect the player's money, fairness and wellbeing.

# 3. Career and Learning

Growth frameworks per role; learning budget; internal talks at the all-hands; rotation opportunities between teams after 12 months. Everyone completes security, AML awareness and responsible-gaming training yearly.
