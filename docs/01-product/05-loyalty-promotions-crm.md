# 1. Goals

Reward play fairly (especially recreational players), build habits around fast formats and scheduled tournaments, and grow through referrals and operators — without encouraging harmful play.

# 2. Summit Rewards

- Points from rake and fees paid (1 point per 1 cent), tiers and weekly cash-back as defined in KP-FIN-04 §4.
- Tier progress bar and weekly payout shown in the app; cash-back paid as withdrawable cash.
- Play Money has a parallel programme with cosmetic rewards (avatars, card backs, table themes) and Play Money chips.

# 3. Missions and Leaderboards

@widths 1.1,2.1,1.8
| Type | Examples | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Daily missions | Play 50 fast-fold hands; win a pot with a flush | Rewards: small cash (Real Money) or points; one set per day |
| Weekly challenges | Cash in 3 tournaments | Tickets to freerolls or target events |
| Leaderboards | Weekly points race per stake band; Spin multiplier leaderboard | Published rules; prizes funded from promotions budget; integrity checks before payout |
| Onboarding journey | First deposit, first tournament, first fast-fold session | Teaches formats; small rewards |

Missions must not require long or late-night sessions; mission design is reviewed by player care against the responsible-gaming checklist.

# 4. Welcome and Referral Offers

- Welcome offer per KP-FIN-04 §5 (deposit match released with rake; tickets to freerolls).
- Referral: both players receive a reward after the referred player verifies and pays a minimum rake; fraud controls per KP-SEC-04 §5.
- Operator skins run their own offers funded by the operator, within network rules (no rake-free games, no rewards linked to specific opponents).

# 5. CRM

- Channels: in-app inbox, push, SMS/WhatsApp (with consent), email.
- Segments: lifecycle (new, active, lapsing, dormant), format preference, stake band, value, language, country.
- Consent: marketing only with opt-in where required; one-tap unsubscribe per channel.
- Frequency caps: at most 1 marketing push per day and 3 per week per player.
- **Exclusions (automatic):** self-excluded, cooling-off, players with active limit hits in the last 7 days, players flagged by the responsible-gaming model, minors, players in jurisdictions where the promotion is not allowed.

# 6. Promotions Governance

- Every promotion has written terms, budget, eligibility, start/end, jurisdictions, and a responsible-gaming and fraud review before launch.
- Terms are published in the app in all launch languages.
- Post-promotion review: cost, incremental activity, abuse detected, player feedback.
