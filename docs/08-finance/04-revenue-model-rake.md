# 1. Revenue Streams

@widths 1.5,3.5
| Stream | Description |
|---|---|
| Cash-game rake | Percentage of each pot that reaches the flop, capped per hand (ADR-0018) |
| Tournament and SNG fees | Fixed fee on top of the buy-in (e.g. 10 + 1) |
| Spin edge | Difference between buy-ins and expected prizes (paytable RTP) |
| FX spread | Spread on deposit and withdrawal conversions (disclosed) |
| B2B | Kilima's share of rake generated by operator players, platform fees |

Kilima does not earn from player losses: the house never plays against players, and rake/fees are the only game revenue.

# 4. Rewards: "Summit Rewards"

A transparent loyalty programme: every unit of rake or fee paid earns points; tiers set a weekly cash-back percentage; missions and leaderboards add variable rewards.

@widths 1,1.4,1,1.6
| Tier | Qualification (points per month) | Rakeback | Extras |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base Camp | 0 | 10 % | Missions, freerolls |
| Ridge | 1,000 | 18 % | Tier freeroll |
| Summit | 5,000 | 25 % | Priority withdrawals review |
| Peak | 20,000 | 35 % | Personal host, series tickets |

- Points = rake/fees paid in cents (1 point per 1 cent), calculated from posted ledger rake only (KP-ENG-08 §4.6).
- Weekly payout as cash (not bonus) through `rakeback` postings; fully withdrawable.
- **Responsible-gaming guardrail:** tier status is never used in messages to push play, and players with active limits, cool-off, exclusion or high risk scores receive no play-promoting communications (KP-LEG-05).
- **Integrity guardrail:** confirmed cheaters forfeit rewards; rewards for accounts under investigation are held.

# 5. Promotions Budget

- Annual budget as a percentage of GGR (planning baseline 20–30 % in the first year including rakeback; target ≤ 35 % all-in), approved by the CFO, tracked weekly.
- Guarantee overlays are budgeted per tournament series; overlay risk is capped per event.
- Welcome offer: deposit match released as rake is paid (e.g. 100 % up to 100 USD, released in 1 USD steps for every 5 USD of rake paid, i.e. 500 points, within 60 days); bonus funds never used for buy-ins in v1 (KP-ENG-08 §4.6).
- Promotions are available only where local rules allow them and are subject to marketing rules in each jurisdiction.

# 6. B2B Revenue Share (baseline)

- Operators receive a share of **net rake** generated by their players (contributed method, KP-ENG-14 §6): typical range 40–60 % depending on volume tiers and who provides KYC/payments.
- Network costs deducted before the share: rakeback funded by the network, network promotions, gaming taxes where Kilima is the payer, certification and platform fees per contract.
- Minimum platform fee per operator per month covers integration and operations cost.

# 7. Unit Economics (planning model)

Illustrative inputs to be replaced with measured data after Beta:

@widths 2.5,1.2,1.3
| Metric | Assumption | Source to validate |
|---|---|---|
| Average rake per cash hand (micro/low stakes) | 0.04–0.06 USD | Beta data scaled to Real Money |
| Hands per active cash player per month | 1,500 | Beta cohorts |
| Tournament fee per active tournament player per month | 3–5 USD | Market benchmarks |
| Rakeback + promotions | 25–35 % of GGR | Section 4–5 |
| Payment costs | 1.5–3 % of deposit volume | Provider quotes |
| Gaming taxes | per jurisdiction | Counsel and tax advisers |

The model in the business case (KP-PRD-01) uses these assumptions with sensitivity ranges; decisions to change rake, fees or rewards are taken with a documented impact estimate on player economy health (winner/loser distribution, recreational player retention).

# 8. Economy Health KPIs

- Rake per 100 hands per stake; share of rake paid by top 1 % and top 10 % of players.
- Recreational retention (players with net loss in month 1 still active in month 3).
- Winner/loser distribution and the share of winnings extracted by the top 1 %.
- Liquidity: average seated players per stake at peak and off-peak, waitlists, Spin fill time.
- Promotions ROI and overlay cost per series.
